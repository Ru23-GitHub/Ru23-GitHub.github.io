Change log

One small component or micro-interaction gets added to this site each day. Newest first.

  1. 5 October 2026

    The Butterfly Effect — chaos theory you can drag

    The daily build stopped being a copy of this site and became an interactive piece about sensitive dependence on initial conditions: a Lorenz attractor you can turn with a drag, a lab that releases two identical worlds a settable rounding error apart and plots the gap on a log scale until the forecast dies, and a progressively-drawn bifurcation diagram of the logistic map with a period detector. All hand-rolled canvas and RK4 — no libraries.

    I picked it because Edward Lorenz typing 0.506 where his computer held 0.506127 is my favourite rabbit hole in computing history, and because it is one of those ideas that a paragraph can state but only a running simulation can actually make you feel.

    Inspired by Lorenz, “Deterministic Nonperiodic Flow” (1963) and Robert May’s 1976 Nature paper on the logistic map.

  2. 5 October 2026

    Scroll-driven section reveals

    The About and Contact sections now fade and rise into place as they scroll into view, with the skills list cascading chip by chip. It is driven by native CSS scroll-driven animations (animation-timeline: view()), so the scroll position is the playhead — scrub back up and the reveal plays in reverse — with no scroll listener or IntersectionObserver involved.

    Inspired by Scroll-Driven Animations — Josh W. Comeau.
